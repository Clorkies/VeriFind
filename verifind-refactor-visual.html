VeriFind — Refactor Visual Aid

From Wallets & QR to
School-Managed Registry

A simplified, accessible approach to campus Lost & Found — backed by blockchain audit trails, driven by campus identity.

Section 01
Old Model vs New Model

❌ Old: Wallet + QR Ownership

Requires a Cardano wallet (Eternl, Lace, Nami)
Student must understand blockchain to use the system
QR sticker encodes wallet address — anyone can attach their QR to your item
Ownership "verified" by QR scan match — easily spoofable
Limited audience: CS / blockchain-literate students only

✓ New: School-Managed Registry

Any student uses their Student ID — no wallet needed
Zero blockchain knowledge required for students or finders
Ownership verified by staff + item description, not cryptographic keys
Human-in-the-loop prevents fraudulent claims
Open to all CIT-U students — no technical barrier
Section 02
Actors & Responsibilities
🔍

Finder

Brings found item to the Lost & Found desk or submits via the public intake form. No account, no wallet required.

🎓

Student (Claimant)

Browses the public board. Submits a claim using their Student ID and a description of their item. Collects in person.

🏛️

Admin (L&F Staff)

Logs items into the registry. Reviews claim requests. Verifies Student ID and item description. Approves or rejects. Releases the item.

🔗

VeriFind System

Hosts the registry and public board. Manages item lifecycle and claim queue. Writes audit trail entries on-chain (admin-triggered only).

Section 04
How Ownership Is Actually Verified

Factor 1 Student ID

  • Ties the claim to a real, registered CIT-U student
  • Admin cross-checks against official school records
  • Creates accountability — the claim is tied to a real person

Factor 2 Descriptive Proof

  • Student must describe brand, color, markings, contents, etc.
  • Details only the real owner would plausibly know
  • Admin physically compares description to the found item

Factor 3 Physical Pickup

  • Student must appear in person at the L&F office
  • Presents their physical campus ID card to staff
  • No remote release — prevents proxy fraud

Factor 4 Staff Judgment

  • Only an admin can finalize a claim — no self-service release
  • All approvals are logged with admin ID and timestamp
  • High-value items can require supervisor dual-approval

Why this is more secure than QR stickers: A bad actor would need to (1) know the item's specific details, (2) possess a valid CIT-U Student ID, and (3) physically impersonate the real owner in front of L&F staff. The old QR model required only knowing the wallet address — which was encoded in a sticker anyone could copy or swap.

Section 07
Blockchain's Narrowed Role

❌ No Longer Used For

ownershipWallet address no longer represents item ownership
qr-verifyQR payload-to-wallet matching is removed
student-txStudents never submit transactions directly

✓ Still Used For (Admin Only)

item.loggedItem registered in system — immutable timestamp
claim.approvedAdmin approves a claim — anchors the decision
item.returnedItem physically released — final audit record

On-chain records are written by the school's admin wallet only — never by students. This preserves a tamper-resistant audit trail that institutional records alone cannot provide, while removing all technical barriers for students.
